Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Overview

Shopify App Cost Auditor ("the Extension") is a Chrome extension that helps Shopify store owners understand their app spending. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

Data Collection & Processing

All data is processed locally on your device. The Extension does not collect, transmit, or store any of your Shopify data on external servers. Your app listings, billing information, and performance metrics never leave your browser.

External Communication

The Extension communicates with exactly one external service:

  • ExtensionPay (extensionpay.com) — Used solely for license verification and payment processing. ExtensionPay receives only the information necessary to manage your subscription status. No Shopify store data is shared with ExtensionPay.

Permissions & Justifications

Permission Purpose
storage Stores your scan results, settings, and license status locally in Chrome.
alarms Schedules automated periodic scans (Pro feature).
activeTab Accesses the currently active Shopify admin tab to trigger scans.
admin.shopify.com Reads app and billing data from your Shopify admin pages.
*.myshopify.com Measures JavaScript performance impact of installed apps on your storefront.
extensionpay.com Communicates with ExtensionPay for license verification and payment.

Data Storage

All data is stored in Chrome's local storage (chrome.storage.local) on your device. This includes detected apps, scan history, user settings, and license status. You can clear all stored data at any time through the extension.

Third-Party Services

We do not use analytics, tracking pixels, advertising networks, or any data collection services. The only third-party service used is ExtensionPay for payment processing.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date above.
